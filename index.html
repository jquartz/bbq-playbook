MENU

Seven smokehouse centrepieces.

The brisket point becomes burnt ends, so one packer delivers two distinct dishes.

01 · Beef

Sliced brisket

7.5 kg whole packer, oak-smoked and long-held.

02 · Beef

Burnt ends

Point cubes glazed with BBQ sauce, tallow and Worcestershire.

03 · Pork

Pulled pork

4 kg boneless shoulder with sweet-smoky rub.

04 · Beef

Short ribs

Six individual ribs, smoky rub with reduced sugar.

05 · Beef

Confit oxtail

2 kg smoked for bark, then submerged in beef tallow.

06 · Grill

Sausages

500 g premium links, lightly smoked and grilled.

07 · Last minute

Bone marrow

Split bones roasted hard and fast, served with chimichurri.

Sides

Full buffet

Mac & cheese, smoky beans, coleslaw, pickles, chimichurri and rosemary potatoes.

Operating principle: the charcoal pit creates smoke flavour. The oven supplies predictability. The gas BBQ handles high-heat and finishing jobs. The hob reheats the oxtail in tallow.
